on Google (Business Profile + YouTube) and inside Instagram/Facebook search.

Honesty rule: CoatCraft is based in Pune and serves Pan-India (facts.json). Captions may say we *serve* an area
("Chakan, Ranjangaon aur Supa MIDC mein service"), never that we *did a project* there or have an office there,
unless Pravin confirms it in `photos/notes.md`.

## Per-network format
- **YouTube Short title** (<100 chars): `<Keyword> in <Area> | <Hook> | CoatCraft Solutions #shorts`.
  Description: first 2 lines carry the keyword + area (that is what search reads), then facts, contact,
  "Service areas: <cluster>, Maharashtra | Pan-India", then 3-5 hashtags. `tags`: 10-15 (service, service + area,
  area + "MIDC", "epoxy flooring Maharashtra", "industrial flooring India", "coatcraft solutions").
- **Instagram**: first line = keyword-led hook (Instagram search indexes captions). Add one line
  "📍 Service: <area list>, Maharashtra | Pan-India". Hashtags: 4-5 service tags + 3-4 area tags
  (#chakanmidc #punemidc #nashikindustry #maharashtraindustry) + #coatcraftsolutions.
- **Facebook**: same keyword-led first line and the service-area line; ~5 hashtags.
- **Google Business Profile update**: 150-300 words, plain text. Open with "<Service> in <Area>" naturally, say
  who it is for, 2-3 facts, the service-area line, and end with the call line. No hashtags, no phone-number
  spam (once is enough), no ALL CAPS.

1. Primary category **Flooring contractor**; add the epoxy, concrete and waterproofing contractor categories
   that Google offers in the category search.
2. **Service areas**: add up to 20, e.g. Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Chakan, Talegaon Dabhade, Ranjangaon,
   Ahilyanagar, Supa, Nashik, Sinnar, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Bhiwandi, Raigad, Satara,
   Kolhapur, Solapur, Baramati, Nagpur, Maharashtra.
3. **Services**: add each service from facts.json with a 1-2 line description containing the keyword.
4. Business description (750 chars): services + industries + "Pune based, serving MIDCs across Maharashtra and
   Pan-India" + phone.
5. **Reviews** are the biggest ranking factor: after every finished job, send the client the GBP review link on
   WhatsApp and ask them to mention the service and the city in the review. Reply to every review.
6. Photos: upload real site photos weekly (before / during / after).
7. Website: one page per main area ("Epoxy Flooring in Chakan MIDC", "... Nashik", "... Supa") linking back to
   the GBP; same name, address and phone everywhere (NAP).
