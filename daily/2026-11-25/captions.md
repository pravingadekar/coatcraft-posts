Epoxy flooring in Ahilyanagar: roller coating or industrial epoxy? CoatCraft Solutions, based in Pune, installs both, and the right choice depends on how the floor is used. Roller coating is a thin-film epoxy/PU coating applied by roller, an economical option for light-duty areas such as light-traffic warehouses, storage areas and back-of-house spaces. Typical thickness is 150-300 microns, installation takes 1-2 days, expected life is 3-5 years with proper maintenance, and the warranty is up to 1 year. Industrial epoxy is a high-strength, seamless coating built for heavy industrial use in factories, warehouses and manufacturing units, with excellent abrasion, impact and chemical resistance. Typical thickness is 2mm to 5mm, installation takes 2-5 days, expected life is 8-10 years with proper maintenance, and the warranty is up to 2 years.
