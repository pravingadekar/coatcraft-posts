# Content pillars (one per weekday)

Every day: 1 carousel (6 slides, 1080x1350) + 1 reel (5 scenes, Rudra voice). Pick the topic for the day's pillar:
the first item in its queue that is not yet in `history.md`. When a queue is used up, start it again from the top
with a fresh angle (different hook and examples).

Keys refer to `brand/facts.json` (`services.<key>` / `industries.<key>`).

## Mon: Service spotlight
One service: what it is, benefits, where it is used, thickness, expected life, install time, warranty.
Queue: industrial_epoxy, pu_flooring, pu_concrete, esd_flooring, chemical_resistant_flooring,
densi_polished_concrete, car_parking_flooring, epoxy_waterproofing, floor_repair, epoxy_grouting, roller_coating,
wall_coating, coving

## Tue: Industry solution
One industry: its floor problems (from the blurb), the recommended services, and why they fit.
Queue: warehouse, pharma, food_industry, automobile, manufacturing, hospital, parking, chemical_plant, electronics,
cold_storage, data_center, commercial, residential

## Wed: Project showcase / comparison
If `photos/` has a photo not yet in `history.md`: build the post around it (scene `photo`), saying only what
`photos/notes.md` says about it. No client names or locations beyond the city.
Otherwise a side-by-side comparison from facts. Queue: Epoxy vs PU Flooring, PU Flooring vs PU Concrete,
Roller Coating vs Industrial Epoxy, Densi Polished Concrete vs Epoxy, Metallic vs 3D Epoxy, Flakes vs Granule Epoxy

## Thu: Myth vs fact / care tips
Common doubts, answered with facts (maintenance, life, cleaning). Queue: "Epoxy floor slippery hota hai?",
"Epoxy sirf factory ke liye hai?", "Floor ki safai kaise karein (system ke hisaab se)",
"Floor jaldi kyun kharab hota hai (heavy load, chemicals, galat system)", "Steam cleaning: kaunsa floor safe hai?",
"Static se electronics ko nuksaan: ESD kya karta hai?"

## Fri: Decorative floors (homes, showrooms, offices)
Queue: metallic_epoxy, epoxy_3d, italian_marble_finish, digital_paper_epoxy, flakes_epoxy, granule_epoxy, table_tops

## Sat: Process & FAQ
Queue: "Naya floor kitne din mein ready?" (install times), "Kaunsa floor kitna mota?" (thickness),
"Kaunsa floor kitne saal chalega?" (expected life), "Warranty kis system par kitni?",
"Floor lagwane se pehle 4 sawaal" (area, traffic/load, chemicals, cleaning method)

## Sun: Brand day
"Kaun sa floor kis jagah?" (industry to service), services overview, why CoatCraft (seamless, easy cleaning,
8-15 saal life*, up to 2 saal warranty*, Pune se Pan-India). Rotate the angle each week.
